## General PTO Accrual

Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of PTO per month (21 days/year), credited at the start of each month. PTO can be requested through the HR portal and requires manager approval.

## Requesting Time Off During an Active Client Engagement

Time off requests that fall within an active, billable client engagement follow a stricter process than routine PTO:

1. Submit the request at least 10 business days in advance (vs. the standard 3 business days) so the engagement lead has time to arrange coverage.
2. The request must be co-approved by both your direct manager and the engagement/project lead for the client account.
3. If the absence is longer than 2 consecutive business days, the engagement lead must confirm a coverage plan (either a backup team member or a client-communicated adjustment to the timeline) before the request is finalized.
4. Requests submitted with less than 10 business days' notice during an active engagement may still be approved at the engagement lead's discretion, but are not guaranteed and should be treated as exceptions, not the norm.

## Emergency/Unplanned Absence

Unplanned absences (illness, family emergency) during a client engagement do not require advance notice — notify your manager and engagement lead as soon as reasonably possible, and the engagement lead is responsible for arranging same-day coverage if the absence is client-facing.

## Unused PTO

Up to 5 unused PTO days roll over into the next calendar year; anything beyond that is forfeited unless local law requires payout.
