## Standard Client Refunds

For standard (self-serve) clients, refunds are issued in full if requested within 14 days of purchase, provided fewer than 10% of contracted service hours have been delivered. Refunds after the 14-day window are issued as prorated credit toward future invoices, not cash refunds.

## Enterprise Client Refunds

Enterprise clients (contract value over $50,000/year) are governed by the refund terms in their Master Service Agreement (MSA) rather than the standard policy above. In the absence of MSA-specific terms, the default enterprise refund rule is:

- Full refund if cancellation is requested before the engagement kickoff date.
- 50% refund of unearned fees if cancellation occurs within the first 30 days of the engagement, prorated against hours already delivered.
- No refund after 30 days into the engagement; remaining contract value converts to a service credit valid for 12 months.

All enterprise refund requests must be approved by the Finance team lead and the assigned Account Manager jointly before processing. Requests are typically resolved within 5 business days.

## Non-Refundable Items

Setup fees, custom integration work already delivered, and third-party licensing costs passed through to the client are non-refundable under any tier.

## How to Request a Refund

Clients submit refund requests through the billing portal or by emailing billing@company.com. Enterprise clients should also notify their Account Manager directly so the request isn't delayed in the general billing queue.
