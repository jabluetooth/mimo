## Background

CloudStore is our primary object storage vendor, used for document and media storage across the platform. The contract was renewed at the start of Q2 with several changes from the Q1 terms.

## Pricing Changes

Q1 terms billed a flat $0.023/GB-month with no volume discount. The Q2 renewal introduces tiered pricing: $0.021/GB-month for the first 50TB, dropping to $0.017/GB-month beyond that threshold. Egress fees, previously $0.09/GB, were renegotiated down to $0.07/GB.

## SLA Changes

Q1 guaranteed 99.9% uptime with no financial penalty for breaches beyond a written apology. Q2 terms raise the guarantee to 99.95% uptime and add a service-credit penalty: 5% of that month's bill credited back for each half-hour increment of downtime beyond the SLA threshold, capped at 30% of the monthly bill.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Q1 contract had no explicit data-deletion SLA. Q2 terms require CloudStore to permanently delete data within 30 days of a formal deletion request, with a signed certificate of destruction available on request — added specifically to satisfy our updated compliance requirements.

## Support Tier

We upgraded from Standard support (24-hour response) to Priority support (4-hour response for critical issues) starting in Q2, at an added flat fee of $1,200/month.

## Net Effect

Despite the added Priority support fee, modeled Q2 costs are projected to be roughly 9% lower than Q1 at current storage volume, driven mainly by the tiered pricing and reduced egress fees.
